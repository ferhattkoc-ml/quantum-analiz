# Code of Conduct

Our project strives to be a welcoming and professional space for engineers and developers to review our showcase and learn about our engineering practices. We expect all contributors to adhere to the following principles:

1. **Be Respectful:** We value constructive feedback and professional dialogue.
2. **Be Constructive:** If opening issues or offering suggestions, please ensure they are actionable and aimed at improving the repository.
3. **Be Professional:** Please maintain a professional tone in all communications, whether in issues, pull requests, or comments.

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by these guidelines. We reserve the right to remove any content that does not align with these standards.
